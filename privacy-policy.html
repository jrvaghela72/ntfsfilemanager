Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version: this app doesn't collect, store, or transmit your personal data or your files to anyone — everything happens on your device.

What this app does with your data

NTFS File Manager is an offline, local file management tool. It reads, writes, copies, moves, deletes, and displays files that already exist on your device's internal storage or a connected USB (OTG) drive. It has no account system, no login, no cloud sync, and no backend server of its own. Your files never leave your device through this app.

Permissions this app requests, and why

  • All Files Access / Storage permissions — required to browse, read, and write files on your device. Requested only when you first use a feature that needs it, not on install.
  • USB device access — requested only when you connect a USB drive, so the app can communicate with it directly.
  • Notifications — used only to show progress for file transfers, compression, and drive formatting, so you can see and control a long-running operation (including from outside the app). Requested only right before the first time it's needed.

None of these permissions are used to collect information about you — they exist solely so the app can perform the file operations you explicitly ask it to.

Safe Folder

The optional Safe Folder feature gates a hidden folder behind your device's own lock screen credential (PIN, pattern, password, or biometric). The app never receives, stores, or has any access to your credential itself — that confirmation is handled entirely by Android's own system security, the same way unlocking your phone works.

Google Play services used by this app

This app uses a small number of Google Play services strictly for app maintenance — none of them are used for advertising, tracking, or analytics of your file activity:

  • Play In-App Updates — checks whether a newer version of the app is available and can offer to download it in the background. This involves your device contacting Google Play; it does not involve any data from your files.
  • Play In-App Review — occasionally, after clear successful use of the app, may offer Google's built-in review prompt. The app never learns whether you actually left a review.
  • Android Vitals (crash reporting) — once this app is published, Google Play automatically collects crash/ANR reports (like stack traces and device info) to help diagnose bugs, the same way it does for every app on Play. This is Google's own service, governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and is not something this app configures beyond what Play provides by default.

Data shared with third parties

None, beyond the standard Google Play services described above, which every app distributed on Google Play relies on at the platform level.

Children's privacy

This app is a general-purpose file management utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
